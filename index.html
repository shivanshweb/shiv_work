Freight Cost Anomaly Detection and Carrier Pricing Analysis

Compared cost per kg and cost per mile across seven carriers in Excel, flagged unusually priced shipments, and built a Tableau dashboard showing where freight spend gets the best and worst value.

DHL had the highest anomaly rate at 18.5%, roughly double USPS at 9.2%. USPS came out as the best value carrier on cost and consistency, and average cost per kg nearly doubled in peak months. Recommended moving volume to USPS, checking DHL and FedEx invoices before payment, and renegotiating peak season surcharges.
