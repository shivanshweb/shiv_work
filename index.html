About

I trained as a mechanical engineer before moving into data. The two fields ask the same question in different languages: given a symptom, what's the actual cause? A machine running hot, or a business metric that moved unexpectedly, both reward the same instinct — trace it back through the evidence rather than the first explanation offered.

I completed a Master of Data Science and Innovation at the University of Technology Sydney, after a Bachelor of Mechanical Engineering in India. In between and since, I've worked across HR data analysis, front-end development, and a clean-energy startup, building habits that carry over regardless of the domain: check the data before trusting it, write down decisions before you forget why you made them, and keep the explanation short enough that someone outside the room can still follow it.
